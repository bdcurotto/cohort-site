Turn a group into a get-together.

Events are how a group becomes a real plan — a place, a time, and an RSVP, attached directly to the group they came from. Everyone in the group already knows who's going before they show up.

  1. 1

    Create an event from a group

    Set a location, date, and time — it's automatically tied to the group it came from.

  2. 2

    RSVP, no guessing

    Members mark themselves as going, so everyone can see the headcount ahead of time.

  3. 3

    Merged groups get events too

    A merge between two groups can host its own joint event, kept separate from either group's regular calendar.

  4. 4

    Find it on the map

    Every event shows up on the Find tab, so it's easy to see what's happening nearby.

Built campus by campus.

Cohort verifies every signup against a real school email, starting with a single-campus pilot at Kansas State University. Everyone you see is actually on your campus.
