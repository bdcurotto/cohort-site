Groups

Real groups, real people.

 Events

A place, a time, and an RSVP — attached right to the group they came from.

 Find

See what's happening nearby.

A simple map of groups and events on your campus — just what's actually around you.

 Messages

Every conversation, one tab.

Direct messages, group chats, and merged-group chats, all together.
