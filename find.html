See what's happening nearby.

Find is a simple map of the groups and events on your campus — kept intentionally simple for now. No noise, no strangers outside your school, just what's actually around you.

Step 1

Start at the state level

Small, rounded bubbles show how many groups are active in each state, at a glance.

Step 2

Zoom into a campus

Keep zooming and individual campuses appear, each showing how many groups are active there.

Step 3

See exactly what's around you

Zoom all the way in and individual groups and events show up right on the map, pinned to real places.

Everything is campus-scoped.

You only see what's happening at your verified campus, not the whole world — no noise, no strangers outside your school. Tap any pin to see the group or event behind it, and join or RSVP right from there.

Find launches simple by design. As more people join a campus, it'll grow into a fuller view of what's around you.

Built campus by campus.

Cohort verifies every signup against a real school email, starting with a single-campus pilot at Kansas State University. Everyone you see is actually on your campus.
