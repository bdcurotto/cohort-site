Real groups, real people.

Groups are the foundation of Cohort — clubs, orgs, teams, or just the people you already know. Create one, invite people you follow, and control exactly who can see it, post in it, or get tagged in it.

  1. 1

    Create a group

    Name it, add a photo, and set who can see it — open to your campus, or invite-only.

  2. 2

    Invite the people you follow

    Add members directly, or open the group up so people on your campus can request to join.

  3. 3

    Organize with subgroups

    Break a larger group into smaller subgroups — a chapter into pledge classes, a team into travel squads — that live right under the main group with their own chat.

  4. 4

    Post, tag, and RSVP — all inside it

    Posts, event invites, and merge requests all stay scoped to the group they belong to, with per-group notification controls.

Built campus by campus.

Cohort verifies every signup against a real school email, live at every campus from day one. Everyone you see is actually on your campus.
